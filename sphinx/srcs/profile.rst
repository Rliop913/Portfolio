Profile
=======

Summary
-------

My work sits between low-level software and research prototyping. I implement
DSP models, move workloads onto GPUs, connect sensors over constrained
networks, and deliver the result inside an interactive runtime. I work across
the complete path instead of isolating algorithms from deployment.

Current research develops a standalone mixed-reality workflow for haptic
